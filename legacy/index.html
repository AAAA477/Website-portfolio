About

I grew up in Ghana and moved to Canada to study computer science at the University of Alberta. I started with Python, got pulled toward the web because it was the fastest way to put an idea in front of someone, and have been building interfaces since 2023.

What holds my attention now is the overlap between application development and artificial intelligence, and closer to the ground, the unglamorous craft of making a page load quickly and work with a keyboard.
